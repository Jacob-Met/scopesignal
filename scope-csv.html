Bring your checkpoints.

Turn your deliverables, amounts and planned evidence into a scope draft, without retyping each row.

Your file stays in this tab until you choose to download the draft. Conversion creates no approvals, recorded events or payment evidence. Nothing is uploaded or saved automatically.

1. Choose the checkpoint CSV

UTF-8, up to 1 MiB, with 1–12 checkpoint rows. Use these three headers in this order:

deliverable,amount,evidence

CSV format and exact text

LF or CRLF can separate records. Double-quote a field containing a comma, a quote or a line break; write a quote inside it as two quotes. A leading UTF-8 BOM is accepted.

Spaces, Unicode, unfinished amounts and literal formulas stay as text. A deliverable is limited to 160 characters, an amount to 64, and evidence to 5,000. Deliverables and amounts must stay on one line. Evidence can contain LF line breaks; embedded carriage returns are refused so the workspace can preserve the original field.

Use only the three named columns. Extra columns, incomplete records and blank records are refused. A final line ending is allowed.
